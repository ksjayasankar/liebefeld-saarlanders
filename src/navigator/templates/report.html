How this report was made

Public pages of the University of Bern institutes and the Diabetes Center Berne were collected, cleaned and indexed. Apertus, the Swiss open language model, wrote each unit summary from that unit's own pages; every summary sentence was checked against the sentences it cites. Leads are ranked by keyword and meaning search, re-scored by a reranker that reads your need and the page together, and chosen so that together they cover all of your needs.
